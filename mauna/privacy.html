Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

Mauna is a menu bar app that runs entirely on your Mac. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no server of its own. We built it so that we never see anything about you.

We collect nothing. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no device identifiers. Mauna doesn't read what you listen to, and nothing it sees leaves your Mac through us.

What Mauna looks at, and why

To know when to quiet your music, Mauna watches a few things on your Mac, in the moment, and keeps none of it:

  • Which app is in front, and which apps are open. This is how a rule such as “pause while Keynote is in front” works.
  • Whether an app is using the microphone or playing sound. This is how “In a call” knows you're on a call. Mauna only checks the yes-or-no flag macOS keeps for each app. It never turns on, records or listens to your microphone or your audio.
  • Your music player's play state and volume. Mauna asks Apple Music or Spotify whether they're playing and sets their volume, with the permission macOS asks you for. It doesn't read song names, playlists or your listening history.
  • YouTube Music in Chrome or Safari, if you choose it. Mauna runs a small script in the YouTube Music tab that only pauses, resumes and lowers the music. It doesn't read the page, other tabs, or what you browse.

What stays on your Mac

Mauna keeps your settings on your Mac, and only there: your chosen player, your rules (the apps you picked and how each quiets your music), fade time, lowered level and shortcut. Deleting Mauna deletes them.

Purchases

Mauna Pro and its free trial are handled by the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we receive no payment details, name, email address or Apple Account. Mauna only asks the App Store whether a purchase exists. That is the app's only network connection.

Email

If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply. We don't add you to any list.

Children

Mauna collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Any change that involved collecting data would come with an app update that says so first.
