Common questions

What does Mauna do?
You pick apps, and Mauna quiets your music while you use them. Each app gets a trigger (when it's in front, while you're in a call, or the whole time it's open) and an action (pause, or lower the volume). When you're done, the music fades back in.
Which music players work?
Apple Music and Spotify, plus YouTube Music in Google Chrome or Safari with Mauna Pro. You can switch players at any time from the menu bar or in Settings › Players.
Mauna says it isn't allowed to control my player.
macOS asks once whether Mauna may control your music app. If you said no, open System Settings › Privacy & Security › Automation, find Mauna, and turn on your player. Mauna's menu bar popover has a button that opens the right page.
How do I set up YouTube Music?
Your browser has to allow Mauna to pause and play the page. In Chrome, choose View › Developer › Allow JavaScript from Apple Events. In Safari, first turn on Settings › Advanced › Show features for web developers, then choose Develop › Allow JavaScript from Apple Events. Keep a YouTube Music tab open while you listen. If you have two YouTube Music tabs, Mauna follows the one that's playing.
“In a call” didn't pause my music.
Mauna treats an app as in a call while it uses the microphone, or once it has played sound for a few seconds. Some call apps let go of the microphone when you mute, so a muted, silent call can look like no call. Unmute, or switch the rule to “In front” if you'd rather it follow the window.
What's free, and what does Mauna Pro add?
Free gives you two rules, the “In front” trigger, pausing, Apple Music and Spotify, and the quiet shortcut. Mauna Pro adds unlimited rules, the “In a call” and “Running” triggers, lowering instead of pausing, YouTube Music in Chrome and Safari, and your own fade time.
Is there a free trial?
Yes. You can try everything in Mauna Pro free for 7 days, once. Nothing is charged when it ends. Your first two simple rules keep working on Free, and the rest wait, untouched, until you unlock Pro.
Is Mauna Pro a subscription?
No. It's a single purchase, once, with no renewal, and every future update is included.
I bought Mauna Pro but it still shows as locked.
Open Settings › Mauna Pro › Restore Purchase. If it still doesn't unlock, check that your Mac is signed in to the same Apple Account that made the purchase, then email us with the date of purchase.
How do I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. We can't issue them ourselves, but tell us what went wrong anyway. If it's a bug, we'd rather fix it.
How do I quit Mauna, or start it with my Mac?
Click the Mauna icon in the menu bar. Quit is at the bottom of the popover, and Settings › General has Open Mauna at login.
What does Mauna need to run?
A Mac running macOS 14.2 or later.

Reporting a bug

If you can, include your music player, the app and rule involved, what you expected to happen, what happened instead, and a screenshot or screen recording.
