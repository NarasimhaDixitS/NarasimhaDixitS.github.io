Support

Questions, bugs and anything that isn't working

Email sn.dixitdixit@gmail.com and you'll get a reply within two working days. Waypoint is made by one person, so your message is read by the person who can fix it.

The fastest route is Settings → Send Feedback inside the app: it prefills your app and iOS version, which is usually the first thing needed to reproduce a problem.

Common questions

Does my data sync between devices?
No. Waypoint keeps everything on the iPhone it was created on. There is no account and no server, which is what lets the app collect nothing about you — but it also means a new phone starts empty, and deleting the app deletes your history permanently.
I deleted the app and lost my tasks. Can you restore them?
Unfortunately not. There's no copy anywhere for us to restore from. Your subscription is separate and does come back — see Restore Purchases below.
What's free, and what needs a subscription?
Today is always yours: you can plan and complete today's tasks, keep one goal, and look back at every day you've already lived, for free and forever. A subscription adds planning future days, editing past days, the Week and Progress tabs, and unlimited goals. Nothing you have already done is ever hidden from you because a subscription lapsed.
How does the free trial work?
Everything is unlocked for 14 days from first launch, with nothing to enter and no card required. When it ends, the free tier above continues — the app doesn't stop working.
I subscribed but the app still shows the paywall.
Go to Settings → Restore Purchases. If it still doesn't unlock, check that the iPhone is signed in to the same Apple Account that made the purchase, then email us with the date of purchase and we'll sort it out.
How do I cancel?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Waypoint → Cancel. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for. You can also manage subscriptions here.
How do I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. We can't issue them ourselves, but tell us what went wrong anyway — if it's a bug, we'd rather fix it.
Why can't I move all my unfinished tasks to tomorrow at once?
That's deliberate. Tracking a missed day only works if the miss stays visible; a button that sweeps yesterday away quietly removes the one piece of information you'd want. You can reschedule any individual task by editing its time.
Can I reorder tasks by dragging them?
No — tasks are ordered by the time you give them, so changing a task's time is how you move it. Dragging was built three different ways and each one had bugs that would have made the day's order untrustworthy, which is worse than not having it.
What does Waypoint need to run?
An iPhone running iOS 17 or later. There's no iPad or Mac version yet.

Reporting a bug

If you can, include what you expected to happen, what happened instead, and a screenshot. If the problem is visual, mentioning which theme and palette you're using helps a lot — several bugs so far have only appeared in one of them.
