Night Train

Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 November 2026

Night Train is a focus timer that runs entirely on your iPhone. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no server of its own. We built it so that we never see anything about you.

We collect nothing. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no device identifiers. Your rides, session names and progress never leave your iPhone through us.

What stays on your device

To work, the app keeps a few things on your iPhone, and only there:

  • Your rides: the session names you type, times, stations, tickets, stamps and certificates
  • Your settings: ride and break lengths, sound, chosen view, train colour, free line, daily reminder
  • A small summary that Night Train's own widgets and Live Activity read to show your ride

If your iPhone backs up to iCloud or a computer, Apple includes app data in that backup under Apple's terms. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Purchases

The 7-day Trial and Lifetime Pro are handled by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we receive no payment details, name, email address or Apple Account. The app only asks the App Store whether a purchase exists. That is the app's only network connection.

Notifications, Shortcuts and Focus

Arrival, break and reminder notifications are scheduled on your iPhone and sent by your iPhone. Siri, Shortcuts and Focus filters run through Apple's system features; Night Train only receives the action you asked for, such as starting a ride.

Email

If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply. We don't add you to any list.

Children

Night Train collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Any change that involved collecting data would come with an app update that says so first.
