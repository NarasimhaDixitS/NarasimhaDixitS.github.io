Waypoint

Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 October 2026

Waypoint is a planner that runs on your iPhone. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no server of its own. This policy describes what that means in practice, including the one place where something does leave the device.

Your tasks, goals, schedule and focus sessions never leave your iPhone. They are stored on the device and are not transmitted anywhere — not to us, not to anyone else. We cannot see them, because there is nowhere for them to go.

What stays on your device

Everything you create in Waypoint is written to a database inside the app's own storage on your iPhone, and to a container shared with the Waypoint widget so the widget can draw your day. This includes:

  • Goals, tasks, schedules and recurring task settings
  • Completion history, streaks and progress statistics
  • Fixed commitments such as work and sleep hours
  • Focus session history
  • Your preferences: theme, accent colour, notification settings

Reminders are scheduled by iOS on the device itself. No notification content is sent through a server.

Subscriptions

Waypoint offers an optional paid subscription. Two companies are involved in that, and only in that.

Apple handles the payment. We never see or receive your payment details. Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

RevenueCat is the service we use to check whether a subscription is active, so that the app unlocks on every device you own and restores correctly if you reinstall. When you open the paid features, subscribe, or restore a purchase, the app sends RevenueCat:

  • A randomly generated identifier for your installation — not your name, email or Apple ID
  • Your App Store purchase receipt and subscription status
  • Basic technical details: device model, iOS version, app version, country and time zone

RevenueCat never receives your tasks, goals, notes, schedule or any other content from the app. Their handling of the above is covered by RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

If you email us feedback

The Send Feedback option in Settings opens a draft in your own Mail app, addressed to us. Nothing is sent until you press send, and the message goes straight from your mail account to our inbox without passing through anything of ours.

The draft is prefilled with your app version, iOS version, device model identifier and which colour palette you have on — because most visual bugs depend on those. It contains nothing about you and nothing about your tasks. You can delete any of it before sending.

If you do email us, we have your message and your email address, and we use them only to answer you.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, telemetry or crash reporting
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No tracking of you across other apps or websites
  • No selling or sharing of any data
  • No profile of you anywhere — there is no account to attach one to

Deleting your data

Deleting Waypoint from your iPhone deletes everything it stored, permanently. There is no copy on a server, which also means there is no way to recover it — so if you have data you care about, export or note it down first.

To delete the record RevenueCat holds against your anonymous identifier, email us at the address below and we will remove it.

Children

Waypoint is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
